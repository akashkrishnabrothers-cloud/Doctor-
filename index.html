Questions

Before you visit.

Who should see a cardio-diabetologist?

People who have diabetes together with high blood pressure, high cholesterol or heart symptoms, or anyone with a family history of heart disease or diabetes. Both conditions affect each other, so they are best looked at together.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Your previous prescriptions, recent blood tests (sugar, HbA1c, lipid profile), any ECG or heart reports, and the names of all medicines you currently take.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking in advance by call or WhatsApp is recommended so you are not kept waiting. Use the form on this page to send your request.

Is this clinic for emergencies?

No. For chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or any sudden serious symptom, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
